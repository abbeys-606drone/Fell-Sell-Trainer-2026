## 🎮 About the Game

Fell & Sell is a first-person action RPG and shop-management roguelike developed by Art Games Studio S.A. It released on August 26, 2026. Players explore dangerous dungeons and forests, fight monsters, gather loot, craft equipment, and operate a growing fantasy shop.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Fell &amp; Sell**. This trainer enhances the single-player gameplay loop by protecting the player during dungeon expeditions, increasing available resources, and simplifying shop progression. It is designed around the game's combat, exploration, crafting, inventory, and merchant systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed single-player experience, faster shop progression, safer dungeon runs, easier crafting, or additional freedom while experimenting with builds and the game's economy.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the world from a first-person perspective. Travel between the shop, the forest, and descending dungeon areas, while deciding how far to push each expedition before returning with your loot.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage weapons, armor, materials, recipes, and dungeon finds between expeditions. Use gathered resources for crafting, place valuable goods in the shop, and rely on the trainer's capacity and resource options when carrying limits slow down progression.

### World & Environment

Fell & Sell combines a shop hub with procedurally challenging dungeon runs and a living forest area. Dungeons contain increasingly valuable loot and dangerous enemies, while forest trips provide opportunities to forage and encounter hazards such as goblin raids and thunderstorms.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is fast-paced, first-person mod-and-slash action against skeletons, goblins, monsters, necromancers, bosses, and other threats. Use the trainer's defensive and offensive options to survive deeper runs, test weapons, and recover more loot.
